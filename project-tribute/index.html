Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

1931 - 2015

Missile Man of India

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and politician who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India, and passed away on July 27, 2015. Dr. Kalam is widely regarded as one of India's most influential scientists and a key figure in the country's space and missile development programs.

Dr. Kalam played a crucial role in India's civilian space program and military missile development efforts, earning him the nickname "Missile

Biography

  • 1931: Born on October 15 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • 1954: Graduated in physics from St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.
  • 1960: Graduated in aerospace engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT).
  • 1960s-1970s: Worked at the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), contributing to the development of India's first satellite launch vehicle and ballistic missiles.
  • 1992-1999: Served as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister and Secretary of the DRDO.
  • 2002-2007: Served as the 11th President of India, known for his humility and dedication to public service.
  • 2015: Passed away on July 27 while delivering a lecture at the Indian Institute of Management Shillong.
